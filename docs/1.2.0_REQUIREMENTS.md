# PHASE 2 — ROYAL ECONOMY FOUNDATION

## 2.1 Goal

Extend the existing treasury so kingdom money has real uses.

The existing economy produces daily kingdom tax income in actual Millénaire coins stored in capital town hall inventories.

Preserve this system unless a verified defect requires correction.

## 2.2 Safe treasury spending

Create a shared server-side treasury spending service usable by both player rulers and NPC rulers.

Requirements:

- Spend real existing Millénaire currency.
- Validate funds before accepting a charge.
- Support the actual Millénaire coin denominations.
- Handle denomination changes safely without creating or destroying value.
- Prevent double charging.
- Prevent free transactions.
- Protect against invalid, forged or duplicated requests.
- Handle full and unloaded inventories.
- Preserve transaction state through reloads.
- Support defined cancellation and refund policies.
- Reject spending on invalid or destroyed kingdoms.
- Never confuse tax income owed to a kingdom with physical coins already delivered.

### Financial design

Money should represent administrative expenditure, construction patronage and shipment logistics, not magical production.

Make costs configurable and clearly documented.

Start with conservative default values and test the relationship between typical daily kingdom income and expenses.

Do not arbitrarily redesign the existing taxation model.

# PHASE 3 — ROYAL CONSTRUCTION ORDERS

## 3.1 Core principle

**Villages remain autonomous. The ruler may occasionally commission one specific building, but does not control the village's normal development.**

This principle is mandatory.

A royal construction order must not convert an NPC village into a player-controlled village.

## 3.2 Player workflow

The ruler opens the kingdom interface and:

1. Selects an attached village.
2. Sees construction options genuinely available for that village and culture.
3. Selects one building type.
4. Sees the payment and eligibility conditions.
5. Pays from the kingdom treasury.
6. Issues a single construction request.

The player does **not** select the building location.

The player does **not** manually place blueprints.

The NPC village must determine the location and perform construction using its own existing Millénaire building system.

## 3.3 Integration requirements

Before implementation, study Millénaire 9.0.3's real building planning, construction queues, automated placement and resource requirements.

Find the safest supported integration point.

A valid royal order should:

- Request a culturally compatible building.
- Be subject to existing building plan and village rules.
- Be scheduled without forcibly deleting normal village projects.
- Allow Millénaire's own builders to work.
- Require genuine construction resources.
- Complete through genuine Millénaire construction.
- Preserve the village's autonomous development.

Do not build a parallel instant-construction engine.

Do not directly create finished buildings to simulate success.

A normal NPC village must continue functioning even when it has no royal order.

## 3.4 Restrictions

- At most one active royal construction request per village.
- Reject buildings that cannot legally or technically exist in that village.
- Do not create inappropriate foreign-culture buildings.
- Do not duplicate buildings where the plans prohibit duplicates.
- Do not force an invalid building location.
- Do not endlessly block natural construction.
- Handle missing resources and unavailable building space.
- Handle ownership changes during construction.
- Handle ruler abdication and kingdom destruction.
- Handle save/restart during every order state.

A royal construction order should have explicit statuses such as:

`REQUESTED`, `QUEUED`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `COMPLETED`, `BLOCKED`, `CANCELLED`, `FAILED`.

Choose the exact status model based on real engine behavior.

Cancellation, payment and refund outcomes must be explicit, consistent and tested.

## 3.5 UI

Integrate royal construction orders into the existing kingdom GUI.

Preserve the functionality of the current kingdom screen. Add a dedicated economy/administration section or tab only when justified by usability.

Show:

- Attached village.
- Available building plans.
- Price.
- Current order.
- Progress/status.
- Reasons an action is unavailable.

All permissions must be checked again on the server.

Add English and Turkish translations.

Checkpoint: `1.2.0-dev.3`

# PHASE 4 — ROYAL GOODS SHIPMENTS

## 4.1 Goal

Allow rulers to order a specified quantity of actual goods transferred between two villages belonging to their kingdom.

**Do not create a mandatory Kingdom Market.**

Do not replace or restrict Millénaire's existing trading economy.

Villages must remain free to trade with independent villages and settlements belonging to other kingdoms according to normal Millénaire rules.

Royal shipments are an additional political logistics ability, not a trade monopoly.

## 4.2 Player workflow

The ruler chooses:

- Sending village.
- Receiving village.
- Product.
- Quantity.

The GUI displays:

- Actual available inventory.
- Amount that may safely be transferred.
- Estimated shipment time.
- Transportation cost.
- Shipment status.

Both villages must belong to the ruler's active kingdom when the shipment is issued.

## 4.3 Real inventories

Goods must originate from real Millénaire village inventories.

When a shipment removes 40 units of wood from Village A, those 40 units must not remain available in Village A's inventories.

When the shipment completes, the exact transported goods must enter Village B's inventories.

No virtual shared stockpile may make every village's goods available everywhere instantaneously.

No free item creation or item deletion is permitted.

Preserve enough stock for the sending village's own construction and production requirements.

Derive available surplus from real Millénaire village data whenever practical.

If the existing API cannot reliably calculate reserves, implement a conservative and configurable availability policy rather than extracting the entire inventory.

## 4.4 Timed delivery — no physical caravan dependency

The first release will use an abstract timed shipment system.

Physical merchant or caravan NPCs are not required.

Delivery time should depend on the distance between villages, using a configurable balance formula.

Transport costs should reasonably reflect quantity and distance.

The server manages shipment status. The GUI only displays it.

Avoid forcing distant chunks to stay loaded.

If source or destination inventories are unavailable, safely postpone the relevant inventory operation.

### Shipment lifecycle and conservation

Design a durable shipment transaction model, with states such as:

`PREPARING`, `RESERVED`, `IN_TRANSIT`, `AWAITING_DELIVERY`, `DELIVERED`, `CANCELLED`, `RECOVERY_REQUIRED`.

The exact implementation is your responsibility.

Consider these failure points:

- Server shuts down before goods are removed.
- Server shuts down after removal but before recording transit.
- Server shuts down when shipment completes.
- Destination storage is full.
- Destination village is unloaded.
- Either village changes ownership.
- Kingdom falls during shipment.
- User requests cancellation.
- The same network request is delivered twice.
- Two shipments reserve the same inventory.
- World or chunk save operations occur in different orders.

Ensure that recovery cannot duplicate or silently destroy goods.

Important: Minecraft SavedData and chunk/container persistence are not inherently one cross-file atomic transaction. Do not make unsupported exactly-once guarantees. Design, test and document the actual consistency and recovery strategy, including difficult shutdown boundaries.

If a fully reliable item-transfer protocol cannot be implemented with the available APIs, do not disguise an unsafe implementation as completed. Record the limitation and use the safest viable supported design.

## 4.5 Economy integration

Royal shipping should spend real money from the capital treasury.

Money represents transportation and administrative costs, not automatic payment for all goods.

Do not create a new independent market currency or mandatory NPC village accounts.

All pricing formulas should be configurable and tested.

Checkpoint: `1.2.0-dev.4`

# PHASE 5 — NPC KINGDOM PERSONALITIES

## 5.1 Fundamental rule

**An NPC kingdom's permanent base personality is determined by the type of its capital village.**

Use Millénaire's real village type metadata.

Do not infer personality merely from a kingdom's name or culture.

Different kingdoms of the same culture may have different personalities because their capitals are different village types.

## 5.2 Accepted personality categories

### Military

Military villages produce military-oriented kingdoms.

Behavior:

- Greater tendency toward aggression and military campaigns.
- Greater preference for military construction orders.
- Higher strategic priority for border security.

### Agricultural

Agricultural/farming villages produce agrarian kingdoms.

Behavior:

- Favor farming, food production and animal husbandry buildings.
- Greater investment in productive rural infrastructure.
- Less aggressive general tendencies, unless threatened.

### Religious

Religious village types produce religious-oriented kingdoms.

Behavior:

- Prefer temples, monasteries and available religious buildings.
- Maintain coherent but not necessarily universally peaceful political behavior.
- Still defend themselves and pursue military construction when needed.

### Craft / Artisan

Craft and artisan village types produce production-focused kingdoms.

Behavior:

- Favor workshops, blacksmiths, crafting and other productive buildings.
- Pay greater attention to shortages and kingdom logistics.
- Prefer economic development where possible.

### Palace / General

Palace-type and general administrative centers produce balanced kingdoms.

Behavior:

- Favor prestige, court, civic and decorative structures when genuinely available.
- Maintain relatively balanced political and economic priorities.
- Avoid an extreme specialization.

## 5.3 Classification requirements

Inspect every relevant Millénaire 9.0.3 village type.

Produce an explicit mapping based on actual game data.

Do not assume that every village type has an obvious equivalent in these categories.

Where categorization is ambiguous, use a justified fallback or documented configuration rather than inventing behavior.

Keep culture-specific buildings culturally appropriate.

Expose the resulting personality in the kingdom GUI and diagnostic commands.

The kingdom's base personality should remain consistent across restarts.

A change of capital or invalid village type must be handled intentionally and tested.

Checkpoint: `1.2.0-dev.5`

# PHASE 6 — DYNAMIC NPC GOVERNANCE

## 6.1 Goal

NPC rulers must make meaningful economic decisions using the same underlying systems as player rulers.

Do not create a separate fake economy for NPCs.

NPC kingdoms must spend actual available kingdom funds.

NPC autonomy must remain disabled for kingdoms that have an active player ruler, respecting the existing model.

## 6.2 Construction decisions

An NPC kingdom may periodically evaluate whether it should commission a building.

Evaluation does not imply an automatic purchase.

Its decision should consider:

- Base kingdom personality.
- Actual available treasury.
- Existing royal construction requests.
- Eligible buildings.
- Village development and real needs.
- Current wars.
- Threatened border villages.
- Existing major expenses.
- A configurable treasury reserve.

The NPC should choose an appropriate attached village and eligible building when justified.

Do not issue random construction orders merely because money exists.

Do not issue excessive orders that suppress normal village growth.

## 6.3 Personality-based investment

During peace:

- Military kingdoms prefer military buildings.
- Agricultural kingdoms prefer agricultural buildings.
- Religious kingdoms prefer religious buildings.
- Artisan kingdoms prefer production buildings.
- Palace/general kingdoms prefer prestige or balanced investments.

These are weighted preferences, not absolute restrictions.

Use need-based decision making to avoid irrational behavior.

## 6.4 WAR-TIME MILITARY INVESTMENT — MANDATORY

**When a kingdom is at war, the probability/weight of commissioning military buildings must increase.**

This applies to every NPC kingdom personality.

The base personality remains unchanged.

Examples:

- An agricultural kingdom normally favors farms but becomes more interested in military structures during war.
- A religious kingdom may prioritize defensive buildings when threatened.
- A military kingdom becomes particularly focused on military development during war.

War-time prioritization must consider:

- Actual war state.
- Military building availability.
- Nearby hostile territories.
- Treasury reserve.
- Existing construction orders.

When the war ends, investment weights return to the peacetime baseline.

Implement configuration-driven, testable weighted priorities.

Do not hardcode arbitrary percentages without a balance rationale.

## 6.5 Military kingdoms and war tendency

Military-personality kingdoms should have a higher tendency to initiate military campaigns or wars compared with otherwise equivalent balanced kingdoms.

Integrate this into the existing NPC autonomy service.

Preserve all existing war eligibility checks, cooldowns, truces, military readiness rules and conquest requirements.

Never bypass safeguards simply to make a military kingdom more aggressive.

Do not turn other kingdom types into permanently passive states.

## 6.6 NPC royal shipments

NPC rulers may arrange shipments to solve real resource shortages.

Example:

Village A has an eligible construction order blocked by missing wood.

Village B has a verified surplus of wood.

The NPC ruler may pay for a shipment if it is economically justified.

NPC shipments must:

- Use real inventories.
- Respect protected stocks.
- Pay normal transport expenses.
- Follow the same shipment lifecycle as player orders.
- Avoid unnecessary repeated shipping.
- Respect the limits and capabilities of the system.

NPCs do not need to micromanage every normal village trade.

Normal autonomous Millénaire trade must continue unchanged.

## 6.7 Balance protections

Prevent:

- Military personality creating unavoidable perpetual warfare.
- NPC rulers spending all savings on decorative construction.
- A wealthy kingdom generating infinite construction requests.
- Multiple simultaneous decisions using the same treasury funds.
- Rapid decision loops after failed orders.
- Forced shipment loops between the same villages.
- NPC commands interfering with player-controlled kingdoms.

All relevant AI economic decisions should be explainable through logs or a diagnostic command.

Checkpoint: `1.2.0-dev.6`
