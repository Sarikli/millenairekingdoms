## Product scope and explicit limits

Includes paid construction/queues/non-centre upgrades, real-goods shipments/icon selection/50-unit protection ceiling, capital income, Ethos-aware NPC investment, conditional unloaded planning, native/strategic war, conserving coin maintenance, village-only Atlas, fully developed newly generated capitals and revised event-first EN/TR chronicles. Ocean scanning and territory geometry remain removed. Existing configured chance/timing overrides remain in force; new defaults do not silently rewrite old world settings.

Actual graphical layout, fonts, scales, input, RTL rendering and online authenticated login were not run.26-locale coverage is not native-speaker approval; the other24 languages retain previous localized event bodies without repeated global boilerplate. The copied normal smoke fixture contains0kingdoms: it proves lifecycle/reopen, not universal populated-save migration. Synchronous financial persistence retains measured slow-storage latency; no power-loss or lag-free guarantee is added. Diplomatic joining is conditional/probabilistic, and previously controlled-hostility war tests do not prove naturally starting realms always expand within a fixed number of days. Back up the whole world; read the [compatibility/downgrade policy](1.2.0_SAVE_COMPATIBILITY.md).

The release is a completed product package with those verification limits recorded. PR3 remains open for review; `main` is unchanged and unmerged.
