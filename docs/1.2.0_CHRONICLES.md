# Authoritative chronicles

This experimental expansion belongs to1.2.0 — By Royal Decree. The original RC.8 artifact/evidence remain preserved. Events use separate per-dimension `data/millenairekingdoms/history` storage; native Millénaire is never edited. [Accepted requirements](1.2.0_RC8_ALPHA_REQUIREMENTS.md), [checkpoint evidence/progress](1.2.0_RC8_ALPHA_PROGRESS.md).

## Facts and presentation

Immutable format1 events derive their UUID from dimension, kind and original operation identity. They retain historical names, participant identities, known location, calendar day/game tick, culture, ethos, genuine known actor, reason/outcome and bounded factual fields. New successful founding starts a recorded history; old worlds get no invented founding dates or backfilled successes. Fallen realms retain their archives. Routine taxes are deliberately absent.

The13 kinds are founding, peaceful incorporation, war, peace, village/capital conquest, collapse, ruler claim/release, construction acceptance/completion, significant investment and delivered shipment. Narrative presentation is deterministic EN/TR, separate from facts; see [style](1.2.0_NARRATIVE_STYLE.md). Names are literal text. No fabricated NPC, dialogue, outrage, ceremony, battle detail or original AI replacement.

## Durable boundaries

Political maximum schema7 adds exactly one `historyCommit` UUID in the same authoritative political snapshot as the transition. Existing1–6 remain readable; a compatible6 world stays6/no eager dirty rewrite until a real live historical transition requires7. History itself stays outside SavedData. Composite player founding validates the whole detached proposal before one live publication, avoiding intermediate success on a failed ruler check.

Own political candidate2 is prepared before publication and confirmed only by exact nonce plus successful original serializer/worker/disk read-back. Confirmation is forced before archive append so later political transitions cannot invalidate a verified backlog. Equal later state/time cannot authenticate an earlier uncommitted candidate. Legacy alpha unconfirmed candidate1 is preserved for review, never guessed committed. Future political schema or Minecraft DataVersion is refused before an observer-triggered save.

Own domain candidate1 binds the exact full original construction5/shipment1 intent before accepted/completed/delivered acknowledgement and requires the corresponding original COMMITTED fee. Construction acceptance follows durable original native pending/building publication; completion follows the established physical blocks/materials/resident persistence proof. They are distinct events. Investment additionally requires a matching actual NPC policy decision. Delivered shipment significance is quantity>=64 OR fee>=8; collection/transit/cancellation is not delivery. Original pre-alpha accepted orders are not retroactively enrolled.

A forced confirmed bit precedes archive append. Recovery reads existing original evidence and never replays a payment, cargo credit, NPC commission or political transition. Ambiguous pre-acknowledgement work stays under existing conservative recovery, with no guessed success/refund/adoption. Actual process cuts before order acknowledgement, after order acknowledgement and after political save verify these explicit boundaries; they do not establish universal cross-file power-loss atomicity.

Existing payment1/construction5/audit2/shipment1 formats and financial/native recovery safeguards remain unchanged. Schema7 worlds require matching alpha builds; downgrade requires the matching whole-world backup, including chunks/entities/native data/domain/history. Never remove only a journal/history folder to downgrade.

## Bounds and failures

Each event is one immutable atomic NBT file. Temp-file force/read-back, atomic rename and directory force acknowledge a write. A per-archive filesystem lock serializes appends; exact retry compares full facts. Unknown versions/keys/types/conflicts remain preserved/refused. History failures are diagnosed separately and do not replay gameplay or invent success; unavailable/full archives can leave events pending and require operator attention.

Startup builds indices once: date/time/ID, kingdom, kind and village. Limit100000 events/dimension, page24 bodies; combined filters select an index before reading bodies. No whole archive rewrite/query-time event scan. Own outbox limits128 political/256 domain/128 legacy; recovery rotates at most4 candidates per200 level ticks. Bounded directory enumeration is not a chunk scan. Full archives refuse further append rather than silently pruning old history. Large-world/full-disk/filesystem-specific guarantees beyond tested bounds are not claimed.

## Reading and navigation

Open **K → History** at the management screen's bottom-left (**Tarihçe** in Turkish). Existing management tabs remain. Choose Kingdom Chronicle, World Chronicle, filters or Political Atlas. Kingdom archive search pages64 realms, including fallen realms. Chronicle pages24 records with type/date filters, previous/next and refresh. An old founding date is explicitly unknown. Back/Esc returns to the actual parent; reading selection/scroll survives unchanged responses.

Click event/place/participant links to open the full-screen atlas with the known historical pin/realm selected. Select a village on the atlas to open its indexed history. Historical date/location and present political ownership are always separate. [Atlas visibility, controls and limits](1.2.0_POLITICAL_ATLAS.md).

Read-only network7 binds connection identity, current dimension, session/serial and strict bounded payloads. Retry replies preserve requests under rate limiting. Hidden locations and foreign economic amounts are redacted server-side. These queries never grant management authority. Client and server must use the same alpha version.

Automated server/model tests cannot establish actual rendering, button position, mouse input, GUI scaling or authenticated login. Those remain explicitly manual/unverified in this cloud environment.
