# Capital village-type ethos / Başkent köy türü karakterleri

The screen heading is **Ethos: %s** in English and **Karakter: %s** in Turkish from RC.6. The five temperament names, existing internal `personality` translation keys/category IDs and all behavior stay intact.

Revision1 is an explicit mapping of the pinned original Millénaire9.0.3 metadata, SHA256
`5882528027ac67e8759db3926ebef36abe2300029526582f80c7a988b583ec62`.
All126 native village JSONs were inspected:37 NPC types mapped below;10 controlled types and79 lone buildings are
explicitly excluded from NPC specialization. The machine-readable audit includes each source path and actual centre
plan in `verification/1.2.0/personality/native-type-audit.json`. It contains our classification/audit, not copied JARs,
assets or full dependency definitions. Relevant runtime coverage must compare the mapping to the actual loaded types.

The existing saved political capital UUID and native village type ID are the persistent source of identity. Lookup is
pure/read-only and deterministic: no random seed, kingdom/village name matching, culture-wide inference or additional
political save field. Political format6 is unchanged. A saved capital change intentionally derives the new capital's
type category; an unchanged capital/type keeps the same category across JVMs. Renames, player rule and war do not
change the base. War-time economic weights are a separate Phase6 policy and cannot rewrite personality.

Unknown/custom/unmapped types, missing capital/kingdom, controlled types and lone buildings use GENERAL with an
explicit diagnostic status and retained identity. They are never silently converted into recognized native types.
Later NPC economics must independently check active NPC rule/current authority; a displayed category grants no power.
No configuration or environment setup changes are needed. Future dependency/type changes require another reviewed
mapping revision and coverage audit rather than guessing a culture or matching arbitrary names.

## Narrative display names (RC.4)

The kingdom screen names the temperament of the realm rather than repeating its native village category. The two languages use the same five identities:

| Native origin | Türkçe | English |
|---|---|---|
| Military / Askerî | Savaşçı | Warlike |
| Religious / Dinî | Gelenekçi | Traditionalist |
| Agricultural / Tarımsal | Barışçıl | Peaceful |
| Artisan / Zanaatkâr | Üretken | Industrious |
| Palace / General / Saray / Genel | Pragmatik | Pragmatic |

Warlike reflects military preference and a greater inclination toward eligible wars. Traditionalist evokes devotion to the realm's inherited religious institutions. Peaceful reflects the existing lower war tendency alongside food/subsistence investment; it is not absolute pacifism. Industrious expresses productive workshops and logistics as a character trait. Pragmatic fits a court balancing several needs with an emphasis on civic investment. These are presentation names, not new behavior rules. Internal category IDs, mapping revision1, fallback status, weights and saved identity are unchanged; operator diagnostics retain the technical categories.

Türkçe: Adlar köyün mesleğini tekrarlamak yerine krallığın mizacını anlatır. Savaşçı askerî yatkınlığı, Gelenekçi dinî kurumlara bağlılığı, Barışçıl daha düşük savaş eğilimini, Üretken imalat/lojistiği, Pragmatik ise ihtiyaçlar arasında dengeyi ve sivil yatırımlara eğilimi ifade eder. Barışçıl krallıklar da mevcut koşullar sağlandığında savaşa katılabilir. Kişilik ve dünya kayıtları yeniden üretilmez.

## Deliberate ambiguous assignments

- Indian palace/autonomous palace, Norman Bourg/Gros Bourg, Japanese seiji and Inuit council are GENERAL: their actual
  court/civic/mixed urban layouts are broader than a dedicated military type. A fortified centre alone is not enough
  to override the complete layout. Japanese seiji contains a pagoda but its centre is the palace and its layout is mixed.
- Byzantine trading uses a kastron but has architect/mine/smelter/workshop/mason/toolsmith/dressmaker production and
  omits several agrarian plans. It is ARTISAN, as are the Seljuk market and Japanese sake-brewery trading types. This
  is a documented closest category for commercial production/logistics; no unsupported sixth personality is invented.
- Inuit hunting is AGRICULTURAL as the closest food/subsistence investment category. Its hunter/food-preparation layout
  is not a declaration of military purpose. Inuit farming stays AGRICULTURAL despite sharing the tannery centre with
  the artisan type: actual farmp/farmw/food layout distinguishes them. This is why centre plan alone is insufficient.
- Notre Dame is RELIGIOUS: its marvel/abbaye/cathedral project context dominates a temporary warehouse centre.
- Rare/attached Indian and Norman hamlet types are mapped explicitly even when normal founding cannot choose them.
  Mapping does not grant founding/independence or bypass any existing hamlet safeguard.

The kingdom screen exposes EN/TR labels. `/kingdoms info <kingdom>` retains OP2 and reports category, mapping revision,
capital UUID, exact type and diagnostic status. No new mutation command is introduced.
